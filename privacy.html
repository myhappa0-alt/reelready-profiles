Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information ReelReady Profiles collects, why, and what we do with it. If anything here is unclear, message us on Instagram at @reelreadyprofiles.

What this website collects

This website itself does not run analytics, does not set cookies, and has no form of its own. Ordering and inquiries run through our order page on Foundable, which has its own privacy practices.

What you send us when you inquire or order

  • Your name and contact details, so we can reply
  • Player information you provide — name, school, position, class year, height/weight, GPA, stats, and college preferences — used to build the recruiting profile and college list
  • Film links you share (for example Hudl, Google Photos, iCloud, Google Drive, or WeTransfer links) and details like jersey number and colors, used only to find your player and cut the reel
  • Payment confirmation from our payment provider — we do not receive or store your full card number

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and deliver your package
  • To confirm your player before editing, and to complete the one included revision
  • To contact you about your own order — not for unrelated marketing

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information or your film.
  • We do not post or feature a player's reel, photo, or name publicly unless that player or parent says yes first.
  • We do not use a customer's film to create examples for other customers.

Who else sees it

Only the services needed to run the order: the order-form provider (Foundable), the payment provider, and the file-delivery service used to send your finished package. Stock photos on this site are from Pexels.

How long we keep it

We keep order information and delivered files long enough to deliver your package, handle your revision, and keep basic business records. You can ask us to delete your film links and player information by messaging @reelreadyprofiles, and we will confirm when it is done.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
