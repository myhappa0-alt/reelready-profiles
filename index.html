What we need from you

  • Film links — Hudl is best; Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud links for phone video. Please don't email video files — they're too big.
  • Jersey number — required. Without it, we can't reliably find your player in full game film.
  • Jersey colors — home and away, so we can spot your player in every game.
  • The 2–4 games to pull from — opponents or dates.
  • Big-play timestamps, if you know them — optional, but they speed everything up.
  • Player info — position, class year, height / weight, GPA, key stats and measurables.
  • College preferences — division level, regions, majors — anything that makes the list of 20 right-fit.

How to send your film

  • On Hudl? Open the game or highlight, tap Share, copy the link, and paste it into the order form. That's the easiest way — nothing to upload.
  • Film on your phone? In Google Photos or iCloud Photos, select the game videos, tap Share, choose "Create link" (or "Copy iCloud Link"), and paste that link into the form. Set sharing to "anyone with the link" so we can open it.
  • No link option? Go to wetransfer.com — it's free, no account, up to 2 GB. Upload the videos, choose "Get a link," and paste the link into the form. Send it the same day you pay: those links expire in a few days, and we download your film the day it arrives.
  • Never email video files — they're too big and usually fail. A link always works.
